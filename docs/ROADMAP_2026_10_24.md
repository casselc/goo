## Product direction

Start with what is happening now: a compact log/span stream, readable times,
ancestry and chosen fields. Narrow it through direct record/chart interaction;
inspect an item; stage a fixed question when deeper analysis is needed; preserve
an explanation and exact evidence; reuse definitions and views. Services,
Operations, Definitions and Saved are alternate entrances to that same loop.
Ordinary OTel data must remain useful without a goo manifest or Go application.

The next work improves trust and continuity in this loop. It does not add another
parallel workbench, a vendor feature checklist, or another immediate picker.

## Later proposals and deployment gates

- **Architecture follow-up:** the pure question/step/link owner extraction is
  delivered as N1 (`9277f5b`) above. Decompose population orchestration only when an actual next
  feature needs that seam; it is not part of N1.
- **Semantic follow-up:** typed numeric-attribute predicates are delivered through
  TF1–TF3 above. Numeric-measure comparisons, floats/decimals, unit conversions and
  additional transforms require separate semantics and Go/reference/SQL parity.
- **Evidence-driven product expansion:** collector/exporter diagnostics require
  an actual instrument source, exact metadata and a demonstrated failure signal;
  record age or missing metrics must not become inferred pipeline health.
- **Product follow-up:** reviewed annotation/training exports need a defined
  vocabulary, membership/leakage policy and actual consumer. Shared collaborative
  drafts need an explicit concurrency and ownership design.
- **Shared deployment gate:** authentication, tenant isolation, quotas and
  production retention/backup policies require a named deployment target.
- **External evidence gate:** live Databricks execution, XTDB external ClickHouse
  ingestion, durable live replay and broader native-metric contracts require
  their actual sources and acceptance environments. Offline tests do not close
  these gates.

Do not introduce DataFusion, a new frontend framework, CEL/KCL runtime, universal
result schema, or a wholesale metadata migration as incidental cleanup. Current
native missing-instance behavior must not be relaxed to an empty identity.
