# Goo roadmap — 2026-10-28

Current priority and bounded acceptance index. The user authorized continuing
through the backlog, reviewing the whole product, revising the roadmap and
continuing implementation. This supersedes competing Next labels in older
roadmaps; their delivered scope and historical acceptance remain valid.

Product purpose: **observe now → isolate a population → follow supporting signals
→ reuse the question or explanation.** Ordinary OTel works before a goo manifest.
Use compact passive components, explicit source owners and honest coverage/units.
See the [whole-product review](WHOLE_PRODUCT_REVIEW_2026_10_28.md) for findings,
current competitor references and research limits.

## Delivered foundation — preserve, do not rebuild

- Stream-first Activity, typed filters/comparisons/explanations, trace inspection,
  resource/service defaults, reusable definitions, saved views and investigations.
- Bounded HTTP/RPC/database/messaging profiles and runtime/host/Kubernetes/resource
  slices through 216; generic startup and native-interval record continuation.
- Go 1.27 baseline, native display/application ESM, JSON-body cancellation,
  family-owned source policies/admission, server-owned snapshots and bounded shutdown.
- Explicit instrumentation Runtime, checked startup/binding and app-owned SDK flush.
- Storage-free native numeric/state Evaluate and explicit lifecycle observation.

These are bounded deliveries, not production readiness, universal convention
support, complete service health or a replacement for ClickStack ingestion.

## Review gate

After R1–R3, review the complete Activity → service/resource → detail → saved or
reused question task again, including mobile, keyboard and delayed/source failure.
Reorder later items if the actual product evidence warrants it. Each implementation
cut gets a source/test/diff review, concise validation, cleanup, commit and push.
Use screenshots and model/property/browser campaigns when they catch a distinct
failure class; do not generate evidence bundles for every internal edit.

## Deferred until a named task or prerequisite

Do not automatically add generalized dashboards, a shared canvas, alert scheduling,
training exporters, RUM/profiling, arbitrary expressions, DataFusion/CEL/KCL,
mandatory XTDB/bitemporal storage or live Databricks execution. Preserve existing
offline translation and annotation capabilities. New OTel convention/resource
profiles need exact source/version/unit contracts and one real user task, rather
than another disconnected panel. Shared production deployment needs a selected
authentication, query isolation, retention and restore design first.
