[previous roadmap](ROADMAP_2026_10_29.md) with the shared-component, product,
chart-platform and oscope/Kindly/Plotje/Portal reviews. The complete decisions,
architecture, examples and planned journeys are in
[View grammar proposal260](VIEW_GRAMMAR_PROPOSAL_260.md). Proposed work below is

and remove superseded paths. There is no legacy-support workstream or compatibility
floor. Units/scope/evidence semantics remain current product requirements.

## Delivered bounded V0 cut: one ordinary breakdown surface

The delivered cut preserves these acceptance requirements:

- Full-population shares need a typed disjoint captured-record partition and exact
  denominator from the same exhaustive GROUP BY read, including missing/empty
  groups. A separate total-count read or max-relative bar is not an equivalent
  denominator. Empty denominators are unavailable; partial/unknown results cannot
  claim full-population shares.
- Local Find/sort retains the original denominator and scale. Distinguish finding
  supplied groups from a scope filter that stages a checked new question.
- Numeric means/support and exact empirical duration quantiles remain intact;
  keep honest table fallback where a signed bar is unsupported.
- Pattern, histogram, status/error-rate and dedicated error-triage paths remain
  explicit specializations until their semantics are represented. An error rate
  needs error/all-captured-span quantities with Unset/unknown retained; do not
  silently drop the existing presenter to fit a count-only primitive.
- Ordinary reruns stay on the canonical surface; facet/group actions stage with
  zero reads, and Run executes. Baseline questions retain their captured scope;
  same-content stale actions remain refused across rerun and visit withdrawal.

268 delivers [status/error-fraction adoption](STATUS_FRACTIONS.md) for ordinary
breakdowns, intervals and summaries. Exact row-local Error/count references and
Error/OK/Unset/Unknown partitions are distinct from full-population count shares;

## Reviews and verification

Each cut needs a concrete real-user task, read/effect counters, negative controls,
shared-component conformance and appropriate property/model checks. Keep browser
and Bombadil completion assertions; preserve documentation frames, not bulky
campaign output. Review the first authored numeric/duration profile across product, architecture
and usability before advancing to contributed templates or whole-screen editing.

Complete-data widgets are a first-class requirement of V0. Completeness is scoped,
aspect-specific and attested by the data path, with explicit acquisition when
needed. A complete aggregate need not carry all raw rows; an exhaustive stored
query does not prove complete upstream telemetry. Live eligibility must be
rechecked as coverage/revision changes.

Every existing chart/widget must be expressible in the canonical data and plotting
grammar with its semantic quality/interaction states intact. V0 establishes a
complete family inventory and typed certainty/uncertainty facets; subsequent cuts
complete that mapping, not just a growing set of unrelated renderer examples.
Numeric exactness, estimation/bounds, coverage/sampling, missing/invalid states,
freshness/time and relationship confidence remain distinct and propagate through
Go transformations. Complete-data requirements are one hard facet of that model.

The final target is the entire UI, not only charts: tables, lists, streams,
waterfalls, inspectors, search/filter/facet controls, summaries, forms, actions and
layout share the canonical model/interaction contracts. Scope-bound variants emit
typed operations; host runtime owns execution/history/subscriptions. Built-in
screens and contributed views use the same public composition language. V0 begins
this with a scope-bound table/facet companion to the chart pilot; whole-screen
expressiveness is a completion gate rather than an optional future plugin feature.
