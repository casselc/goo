## Review proposals kept conditional or deferred

Broker backlog/age remains useful research, requiring a pinned real exporter,
metric/resource grain, units/temporality, duplicate-observer rules and an actual
fixture. A short accept/defer contract may inform later depth; processing spans
and demo queue attributes cannot establish generic broker health. This research
does not block 370 or the native-view readiness gate.

Review-task enumeration, authenticated reviewer assignment/global consensus,
exemplars, alert delivery and Databricks lowering retain their separate explicit
contracts. No new backend, ontology, universal navigation manager or compatibility
layer follows from this checkpoint. Go 1.27.3 is already pinned; use current
runtime/stdlib features when a concrete change benefits from them rather than
creating an upgrade or advanced-type-machinery queue.

## Lessons carried forward

Measure at real entry points with independent identity/membership oracles.
Disable intrusive DOM tracing for latency decisions, preserve source hashes and
separate handler/build work, frame opportunity and source/driver wall time.
Small shared-host samples are diagnostic budgets, not universal service levels.
Presentation limits never become measurement limits. Preserve current action
leases, focus and source/read clocks across local rendering and child returns.
