## Product decision

current 430 roadmap carry this sequence as the next work. Architecture and UX
cross-reviewed it without blockers; mode-switch/diagnostic wording and selection

Goo lets an investigator watch telemetry, scope a population, inspect evidence, ask a fixed question, and preserve/reuse its meaning. Code-managed definitions and interactive work share typed meaning. CEL should become an optional contextual representation of a filter inside this journey. General OTel data remains the entry point; neither a demo business domain nor a language editor becomes the organizing interface.

Adopt the existing 436 consolidation as the next numbered milestone. Give it the finite ordered slices below; do not start a competing CEL platform roadmap. Complete aggregate-selection and held-return consolidation first, then use its ordinary staging and ownership contracts for the bounded language bridge. Keep 427–429 preservation contracts pending and reuse their owners where authoring persistence intersects them. Slice completion must not falsely declare those older contracts delivered.

The first app language supports the pilot's fixed-field intersection, not every existing visual predicate. It may retain any admitted parent, including unsupported attributes/presence/numeric bindings, and edit a supported additional condition. Fully supported owned conditions can switch between Conditions and CEL. This is not Convert all Filters. A whole-predicate replacement is available only after an explicit scope choice and complete representability check. Additional language coverage, native metric execution and whole-query builders are not prerequisites.

## Reconciled decisions and apparent conflicts

- **Refine versus replace:** a contextual gesture captures an immutable complete parent and creates one owned refinement slot. Text edits replace that slot against the original parent; they never apply successively to the last refined result. Clearing it restores the parent. Deliberate editing of existing conditions adopts a specific owned slot/base revision; clearing means removing that owned predicate, subject to complete-question validation. No AST-subtree search guesses ownership. The ordinary resulting question remains executable authority.
- **Identity versus provenance:** use current validated family identity, preserving predicate branch order. Do not claim logical theorem equivalence or hash source text as population identity. Keep exact authored bytes separately from canonical generated text. A comment/format edit can change authoring revision without manufacturing a different executed population. Helper spellings that actually lower to the same admitted structure share semantic identity; arbitrary equivalent Boolean formulas are not promised equal.
- **Pair validation versus historical reopen:** validate source/AST pairing when an authoring revision is created or imported under its declared supported compiler contract. Reopen executes/displays retained admitted AST; it never recompiles historical text with today's library. An unavailable authoring compiler prevents reauthoring until explicit upgrade preview, not inspection of old meaning. Do not accept an unverifiable imported source/AST pair as checked provenance; allow ordinary typed intent only through its existing admission path, explicitly without an admitted authoring claim.
- **Language breadth:** the language review's P0 completeness requirement applies to a general editor. The bounded bridge instead checks every attempted conversion and refuses losslessly. Presence-aware attributes and exact numeric/derived-field vocabulary stay a subsequent decision, not hidden prerequisites or silent approximations.
- **Capabilities:** distinguish CEL checking, family lowering, adapter execution support and live source readiness. Planning is source-free; source readiness can require explicit bounded metadata reads under its existing owner. Do not relabel such reads as compilation or automatically launch them on typing.
- **Family unification:** share authoring diagnostics, identity/binding conventions and supplied scope presentation. Keep record, native measurement, lifecycle, prepared-subject and view contracts closed and separately validated. A small record planning result need not introduce empty speculative union arms now. Broader family protocol changes require an actual second consumer.

These decisions reconcile the reviews without requiring a new user preference. Prerelease freedom permits replacing weak internal/wire shapes and all their callers together. It does not permit rewriting the meaning or custody of retained evidence.

## Semantics to carry through every boundary

Clocks stay distinct: log/span event bounds, rolling Activity intent, fixed evaluation anchor, source read time and immutable capture/custody time. Compile/stage cannot consult wall-clock time to move a fixed parent. A previous fixed plan does not authorize future rolling polls. Saved relative intent explicitly rebinds time; frozen evidence does not.

Population grain stays named: logs, spans, reported trace IDs, server-call spans, native streams and declared subjects are different populations. Trace-search conditions remain independent EXISTS, not ancestry. Nearby records are contextual; exemplars are reported associations. Source totals describe stored population, Find describes held records, and successful reads do not prove unsampled upstream completeness.

Quality stays typed: empty, zero, pending, refused, invalid, unknown, stale and unavailable are distinct. Preserve native counter temporality/resets/gaps, histogram representation/units and lifecycle knowledge cutoff. A record filter cannot manufacture metric health or exclude malformed subject rows before their family validation. Valid OTel without `service.instance.id` must be described as an evaluator-policy restriction where applicable, never invalid telemetry.

Budgets remain the pilot's actual contracts: 4,096 expression UTF-8 bytes, 65,536 bytes per referenced string, 1,000,000 CEL work units plus parser limits and combined ordinary 32-node/depth-4 budget. Test boundaries/one-over, Unicode, NUL-literal refusal, SQL-looking literal parameters and exact uint64 above JavaScript precision. Work units do not establish arbitrary host-function memory or wall-time safety; reviewed bindings remain pure, bounded and I/O-free.

## Add, replace, remove and defer

Add only the domain refinement owner, structured diagnostics/bindings, bounded printer, revision-safe authoring state and existing-runtime planning transport needed by the finite journey. A logical field catalog may replace duplicated CEL/domain fixed-field declarations in 436.2 where this directly establishes capability truth; physical column mappings remain in source adapters. Validate complete field/type/signal coverage rather than invent a registry.

Replace JSON cloning in the touched domain path, error-prose inspection, successive-refinement editing and duplicate scope/status presentation. Retire obsolete internal forms and update their callers together when replaced; no compatibility floor requires permanent pilot-wire support. Do not undertake a wholesale migration of every unrelated DTO/error/clone.

Remove proposed CEL-only execution/preservation controls, silent source-to-AST dual authority and any blanket “visual/text round trip” claim. Replace that claim with supported-subset conversion plus explicit lossless refusal. Keep passive display components and existing source/custody authorities.

Defer guarded qualified attributes until an absent/empty/value/malformed truth table and actual CEL/AST/SQL parity are accepted. Follow with exact declared numeric binding states/comparisons, versioned derived identities and exact duration/profile helpers only when a specific journey needs them. A common measure/projection descriptor is useful later when it removes actual package/view duplication; do not block the fixed-predicate bridge on it.

Keep generic exact native-stream evaluation a separate architecture decision: full resource/scope/instrument/attribute identity, no synthetic instance and no stream merge. Keep ancestry/joins, SLO health policies, arbitrary UDFs/plugins, whole-query CEL/KCL selection, dynamic maps, unknown activation, Databricks record lowering, durable readiness caching and compilation pools outside this release. Existing prepared-fact Databricks support remains an explicit different family. No cache/pool optimization without a measured serving workload.
