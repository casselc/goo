## Product judgment

The investigation loop is useful and increasingly connected. Activity starts with
current records; a checked observed service now stages its context without
silently changing time; default/team views support facets, charts, records,
cohorts, trace continuation and configuration preservation. The highest immediate
obstacle is still seeing the actual plotted distinction, rather than discovering
another capability or learning another builder.

Choose **one chart-first rendering outcome** for 317. It must show the real
parent route counts and selected-cohort count within the narrow viewport, not
merely make a chart container intersect it. This is a finite shared presentation
cut over existing grammar/data, not another widget/profile. A 30px improvement
that leaves plotted rows hidden would not complete the task.

## Ranked next outcome: chart first, options and detail second

315 materially shrank the two-value facet to 198/217px and improved context, but
its final narrow parent chart starts at 867px in an 844px viewport. The cohort
chart's 140.9px intersection contains title/read facts rather than a plotted bar.
This remains a real task problem: the user cannot eyeball the relation the view
is supposed to explain. The controls and certainty are valuable; their current
stacking is the issue.

The concrete seam is `scoped-breakdown.mjs`'s qualified context and always-open
View/Measure/Scale/Stage options, followed by `scope-view.mjs`'s title/state/read
clock, quality tags, schema/denominator and actual marks. Consolidate this seam
rather than style each service screen independently.

Use one native **Chart settings** disclosure for existing kind, measure, scale,
order/completeness and the caller's existing Find control. Keep the same handlers
and source/query owner. Place the chart before settings; put the existing Stage /
Inspect selection actions adjacent after the plot. Do not create a second settings
panel, new display schema or component-private filtering language.

## Minimum visible certainty

Keep the literal/authored title and actual qualified grouping/measure/unit visible.
Retain the frame's observed, partial, unknown, no-data or unavailable state and
human UTC capture/read clock. Show captured extent/value knowledge and upstream
sampling meaning compactly, plus the relevant population denominator beside the
values. Partial support, estimated values, unknown sampling, stale/unknown/rolling
freshness, changed/error states and completeness refusals must never exist only
inside a closed disclosure.

One wrapping line such as **complete groups · derived values · unknown upstream
sampling** can replace several default tag rows. The redundant fixed snapshot
tag may move to detail when the visible captured clock already establishes a
fixed observation; nonfixed freshness stays visible. Native **Read facts** can
hold verbose origin/schema/method/full quality and exact clock precision. Keep
unknown geometry unavailable and values inspectable; never turn uncertainty into
zero or hide a warning to meet a height target.

Exact/source facts and settings must open by keyboard/touch without source IO.
Local Find keeps its current supplied-data meaning. The plot can become denser
without shrinking its hit targets or inventing metric support/denominators.
