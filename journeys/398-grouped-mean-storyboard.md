# Reuse mean span duration over time

This executable [actual-source journey](398-grouped-mean.spec.js) follows one
ordinary span population without a Goo manifest. Execution results belong in
[validation](../VALIDATION.md), with measured scope and limits.

1. Run raw spans grouped by a literal attribute in fixed one-second intervals.
   Missing, present-empty, `雪` and `api` are four distinct categories. The exact
   query starts 137ms after a UTC second and ends with a 500ms final interval.
   Every populated cell retains exact duration sum/count; mean uses their ratio.
2. Switch to Heatmap and find `雪`. The full dataset and common duration scale
   remain unchanged. Zero captured spans show unavailable duration. A real span
   with zero duration remains a valid zero mean.
3. **Customize grouped timeline** stages the complete question and selected mean
   presentation with no child evidence. Only **Run population view** reads data.
4. Switch among Small multiples, Heatmap and Table, filter locally, and save.
   Preview a known-zero-count cell and stage its exact group/window in a nested
   population. Its mean remains unavailable, not zero latency. Back restores the
   held parent without a source read, then Back to question restores the origin.
5. Reopen the saved configuration without reading telemetry. Inspect and Apply
   its composition in a different compatible span population; explicit Run
   reads that destination's own data. Its 4.7-second window ends with a 700ms
   bin. Full destination scope, environment policy and page limit are preserved.
6. Read the same duration display at 390px. Table overflow stays internal.

The independent Bombadil companion exercises source → Customize → explicit Run →
Heatmap → unavailable-duration cell selection → held Back. It asserts the mean
measure and exact source questions, not pixels. Independent raw records and
rational arithmetic check each supplied cell; displayed rounded labels are not
used as numerical oracles. Percentiles and averaged means are outside this cut.
