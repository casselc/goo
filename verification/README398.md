# 398: reusable grouped mean duration

The pinned `grouped-duration-mean-v1` profile reuses raw-span duration means in
the existing grouped timeline/records composition. Every populated cell retains
exact duration sum and contributing count. An empty cell has known zero captured
spans but unavailable duration; a captured zero-duration span has a valid zero
mean. No percentiles or averaged means are admitted.

binary, Node 24 and loopback/browser access:

```sh

GOO_GENERIC_BINARY=/tmp/goo-398-reviewed-goo NO_PROXY=127.0.0.1,localhost npx playwright test journeys/398-grouped-mean-actor.spec.js --workers=1
```
